## 1. Executive Summary & Catalog Taxonomy

The Engineering Physics Component Library is an interactive, standalone simulation suite designed to provide pedagogical intuition and numerical rigor across the applied physical sciences. Prior to this expansion milestone (M3), the repository comprised **348 simulation models distributed across 23 canonical engineering disciplines**.

While these 348 models established a robust structural foundation within the Figma Marketing Design System (Inter typography, pill controls, centered canvas viewport, and pastel color-block containers), an exhaustive survey revealed substantial curriculum voids. These voids manifested in two distinct structural forms:
1. **Intra-Discipline Canonical Gaps**: Foundational undergraduate and graduate topics within the 23 existing disciplines were either omitted, truncated to static geometric drawings, or lacking numerical dynamical fidelity (e.g., boundary layer stall in aerospace, Hodgkin-Huxley gating in biomedical, and tuned mass dampers in civil engineering).
2. **Macro-Discipline Structural Gaps**: Entire cornerstone domains of modern applied engineering physics were unrepresented as independent catalog disciplines—most notably **Plasma Physics & Magnetohydrodynamics**, **Astrodynamics & Orbital Mechanics**, **Geophysical Engineering & Seismology**, **Cryogenic Engineering & Superconductivity**, and **Quantum Engineering & Qubit Dynamics**.

To resolve the macro-discipline deficit, **50 new canonical interactive simulation models** have been authored, implemented, and fully validated across 5 newly established disciplines (10 models each):
- `models/plasma_physics/` (Models `plasma_001` through `plasma_010`)
- `models/astrodynamics/` (Models `astro_001` through `astro_010`)
- `models/geophysical_engineering/` (Models `geo_001` through `geo_010`)
- `models/cryogenic_engineering/` (Models `cryo_001` through `cryo_010`)
- `models/quantum_engineering/` (Models `quantum_001` through `quantum_010`)

Every single model adheres strictly to the 6-Gate Automated Validation Harness (`scripts/verify_models.py`), achieving a **100.0% pass rate (50/50 models passing all 6 validation gates)**. With this expansion, the catalog expands from 348 to **398 interactive models across 28 engineering disciplines**.
