Superconducting Transmon Qubit Anharmonicity

Quantum Engineering

How It Works

The transmon qubit operates in the large Josephson-to-charging energy ratio regime (E_J / E_C ≫ 1), drastically reducing sensitivity to low-frequency charge noise while maintaining weak negative anharmonicity α. The non-linear Josephson inductance provides a cosine potential well that pulls higher energy eigenstates closer together, shifting the |1⟩→|2⟩ transition frequency below the computational |0⟩→|1⟩ frequency to prevent multi-level leakage.

Governing Equation
H = 4E_C · (n̂ - n_g)² - E_J · cos(φ̂) , α = E₁₂ - E₀₁ ≈ -E_C
