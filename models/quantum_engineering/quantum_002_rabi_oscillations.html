Driven Two-Level System Rabi Oscillations

Quantum Engineering

How It Works

When a monochromatic oscillating drive field couples to a two-level quantum system near resonance, probability amplitude coherently transfers between the ground state |g⟩ and excited state |e⟩. Off-resonance driving at detuning Δ introduces a generalized effective Rabi frequency Ω_eff = √(Ω_R² + Δ²), which accelerates oscillation rate while suppressing the maximum peak excitation probability to (Ω_R / Ω_eff)².

Governing Equation
$$ P_e(t) = \left(\frac{\Omega_R^2}{\Omega_{\text{eff}}^2}\right) \sin^2\left(\frac{\Omega_{\text{eff}} t}{2}\right) \quad\vert\quad \Omega_{\text{eff}} = \sqrt{\Omega_R^2 + \Delta^2} $$
