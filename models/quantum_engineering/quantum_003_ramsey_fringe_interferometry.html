Ramsey Fringe Interferometry & Dephasing

Quantum Engineering

How It Works

The Ramsey separated oscillatory fields sequence measures qubit transition frequencies and transverse decoherence time T₂*. An initial π/2 pulse creates an equal superposition on the Bloch equator, followed by free precession for delay duration T where environmental fluctuations accumulate relative phase Δω·T. A second π/2 pulse converts accumulated phase into population imbalance, yielding oscillatory fringes with decaying contrast.

Governing Equation
P_e(T) = 1/2 · [1 + exp(-T / T₂*) · cos(Δω · T + Δφ)]
