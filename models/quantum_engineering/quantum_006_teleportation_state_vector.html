Quantum Teleportation Protocol & Entanglement

How It Works

Quantum teleportation transfers an unknown single-qubit quantum state |ψ⟩ from sender Alice to receiver Bob without transmitting the physical carrier. Alice and Bob share an entangled Einstein-Podolsky-Rosen (EPR) Bell pair |Φ⁺⟩. Alice performs a joint Bell-state measurement on |ψ⟩ and her entangled half, collapsing Bob's qubit into one of four unitary transformations of |ψ⟩, which Bob perfectly reconstructs upon receiving two classical bits.

Governing Equation
$$ |\psi\rangle_A \otimes |\Phi^+\rangle_{BC} = \frac{1}{2}\sum_{k=0}^3 |B_k\rangle_{AB} \otimes (\sigma_k |\psi\rangle_C) \quad\vert\quad \sigma_k \in \{I, X, Z, XZ\} $$
