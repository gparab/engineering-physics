Mach-Zehnder Interferometer & Quantum Eraser

Quantum Engineering

How It Works

In a Mach-Zehnder interferometer, single photons take two indistinguishable paths, producing spatial interference fringes at output detectors. Placing orthogonal polarizers (horizontal in path 1, vertical in path 2) marks which-way path information, destroying quantum interference (visibility V = 0). Inserting a diagonal 45° polarizing analyzer before the detectors erases the which-way marker, restoring wave interference fringes.

Governing Equation
I(Δφ) = I₀ · [1 + V · cos(Δφ)] , V = 2|⟨ψ_H|ψ_V⟩| / (⟨ψ_H|ψ_H⟩ + ⟨ψ_V|ψ_V⟩)
