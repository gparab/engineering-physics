Hahn Spin Echo & Decoherence Refocusing

Quantum Engineering

How It Works

Static magnetic field inhomogeneity causes transverse magnetic moments in an ensemble of spins to dephase rapidly with characteristic decay time T₂* < T₂. Erwin Hahn discovered that applying a resonant π refocusing pulse at delay time τ flips the relative phase of each precessing spin packet. Because faster and slower spins continue precessing in the same direction, they re-converge at time 2τ, creating a coherent spin echo revival.

Governing Equation
M_xy(2τ) = M₀ · exp(-2τ / T₂) · |∑_k exp(i Δω_k (2τ - 2τ))| = M₀ · exp(-2τ / T₂)
