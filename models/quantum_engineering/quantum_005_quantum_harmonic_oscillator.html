Quantum Harmonic Oscillator & Coherent Wavepacket

Quantum Engineering

How It Works

A harmonic trap potential V(x) = 1/2 mω²x² possesses equidistant stationary energy eigenstates with Hermite-Gaussian spatial distributions. A displaced Glauber coherent state |α⟩ forms a minimum-uncertainty Gaussian wavepacket that oscillates back and forth at the classical oscillator frequency ω without changing its spatial width, demonstrating the quantum-classical correspondence principle.

Governing Equation
$$ i\hbar \frac{\partial \psi}{\partial t} = \left[-\frac{\hbar^2}{2m}\frac{\partial^2}{\partial x^2} + \frac{1}{2}m\omega^2 x^2\right]\psi \quad\vert\quad \langle x \rangle(t) = x_0 \cos(\omega t) $$
