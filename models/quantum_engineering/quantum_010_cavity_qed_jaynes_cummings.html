Cavity Quantum Electrodynamics (Jaynes-Cummings Model)

Quantum Engineering

How It Works

The Jaynes-Cummings model governs the fundamental quantum interaction between a quantized single-mode electromagnetic cavity field and an artificial two-level atomic dipole. In the strong coupling regime (g ≫ κ, γ), energy coherently swaps between atomic excitation and single photons at the vacuum Rabi frequency 2g. The coupled system forms dressed states |±, n⟩ with energy splitting 2g√(n+1), exhibiting vacuum Rabi splitting in transmission.

Governing Equation
H = ℏω_c · a† a + 1/2 ℏω_a · σ_z + ℏg · (a† σ₋ + a σ₊) , Ω_n = 2g √(n + 1)
