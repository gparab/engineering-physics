Radial Darcy Reservoir Flow & Wellbore Drawdown

Mining & Petroleum Engineering

How It Works

Fluid drainage toward a vertical wellbore penetrating a porous petroleum reservoir is governed by the radial diffusivity equation derived from Darcy's law and fluid mass conservation. Radial convergence of streamlines creates a logarithmic pressure drawdown funnel centered at the wellbore. The steady-state production rate obeys the Thiem-Dupuit radial flow formula.

Governing Equation
$$ \frac{1}{r}\frac{\partial}{\partial r}\left[r \frac{\partial P}{\partial r}\right] = \frac{\phi \mu c_t}{k} \frac{\partial P}{\partial t} \quad\vert\quad q = \frac{2\pi k h (P_e - P_{wf})}{\mu \ln(r_e / r_w)} $$
