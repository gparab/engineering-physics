Kozeny-Carman Porosity-Permeability Network

Mining & Petroleum Engineering

How It Works

Fluid flow through interconnected sedimentary rock pore networks is characterized by absolute permeability and effective porosity. The semi-empirical Kozeny-Carman equation treats the porous medium as an ensemble of tortuous capillary conduits, relating permeability to porosity, specific surface area, and pore throat tortuosity.

Governing Equation
$$ k = \frac{\phi^3}{c \tau^2 S_v^2 (1 - \phi)^2} \quad\vert\quad v = \frac{k}{\mu} \nabla P $$
