How It Works

Earth's magnetic field is sustained by a self-exciting magnetohydrodynamic geodynamo in the liquid iron-nickel outer core. Thermal and compositional buoyancy forces drive vigorous convection, which is twisted into aligned helical fluid columns by the Coriolis force. This helical motion converts kinetic energy into magnetic energy through electromagnetic induction, generating a dominant dipolar magnetic field.
