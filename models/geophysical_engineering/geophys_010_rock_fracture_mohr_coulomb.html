Rock Fracture Mohr-Coulomb Failure Envelope

Geophysical Engineering

How It Works

The Mohr-Coulomb failure criterion dictates the shear fracturing of brittle rock under triaxial compressive stress. Shear failure occurs when the internal shear stress on any plane overcomes rock cohesion c plus frictional sliding resistance proportional to the normal stress σ_n. On Mohr's stress diagram, failure initiates when the stress circle touches the linear failure envelope at conjugate fracture angles θ = 45° + φ/2.

Governing Equation
τ_failure = c + σ_n · tan(φ) , σ₁ = 2c · tan(45° + φ/2) + σ₃ · tan²(45° + φ/2)
