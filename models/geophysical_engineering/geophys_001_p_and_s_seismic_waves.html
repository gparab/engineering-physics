P-Wave & S-Wave Seismic Body Wave Propagation

Geophysical Engineering

How It Works

Seismic body waves travel through Earth's elastic interior following an earthquake rupture. Primary (P) waves are longitudinal compressional waves where rock particles oscillate parallel to the wave propagation direction. Secondary (S) waves are transverse shear waves where particles oscillate perpendicular to the path. Because rocks resist compression far more strongly than shear deformation, P-waves travel approximately 1.7 times faster than S-waves, arriving first on seismographs.

Governing Equation
$$ v_P = \sqrt{\frac{K + \frac{4}{3}\mu}{\rho}} \quad\vert\quad v_S = \sqrt{\frac{\mu}{\rho}} $$
