Tsunami Shallow-Water Shoaling & Green's Law

Geophysical Engineering

How It Works

Tsunami waves propagate across open oceans as shallow-water gravity waves with phase speeds v = √(gh). Because open ocean depth (h ≈ 4000 m) yields wave speeds exceeding 700 km/h, conservation of energy flux dictates that when waves encounter shallow coastal shelves, their speed drops abruptly while wave height surges according to Green's law: (A₂ / A₁) = (h₁ / h₂)^(1/4).

Governing Equation
v = √(g · h) , (A₂ / A₁) = (h₁ / h₂)^(1/4) [Green's Law]
