Seismic Refraction Survey & Snell's Law

Geophysical Engineering

How It Works

Seismic refraction surveying maps subsurface geological layers by measuring the travel times of refracted acoustic waves. When a seismic source fires on the surface, waves penetrate downward and critically refract along higher-velocity bedrock interfaces according to Snell's law (sin θ_c = v₁ / v₂). These critically refracted head waves continuously generate wavefronts back to an array of surface geophones.

Governing Equation
$$ \sin(\theta_c) = \frac{v_1}{v_2} \quad\vert\quad t(x) = \frac{x}{v_2} + \frac{2 z_1 \sqrt{v_2^2 - v_1^2}}{v_1 v_2} $$
