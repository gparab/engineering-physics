Borehole Electrical Resistivity Well Logging

Geophysical Engineering

How It Works

Electrical resistivity well logging measures the electrical resistivity of geological formations surrounding a borehole to identify rock types and fluid contents. Low resistivity characterizes saline water-bearing sands and clay-rich shales, while high electrical resistivity indicates non-conductive hydrocarbon reservoirs (crude oil or natural gas). Archie's empirical equation translates the measured resistivity into quantitative hydrocarbon saturation.

Governing Equation
R_a = 4π · s · (ΔV / I) [Wenner] , S_w = ((a · R_w) / (Φ^m · R_t))^(1/n) [Archie]
