Rayleigh & Love Seismic Surface Waves

Geophysical Engineering

How It Works

Seismic surface waves are guided elastic waves confined to Earth's surface that produce the strongest ground motions during earthquakes. Rayleigh waves cause retrograde elliptical particle rolling in the vertical-radial plane, decaying exponentially with crustal depth. Love waves form when horizontally polarized shear waves are trapped within a superficial crustal waveguide via total internal reflection, creating purely horizontal transverse ground shaking.

Governing Equation
$$ v_R \approx 0.92 \, v_S \quad\vert\quad v_{S1} < v_{\text{Love}} < v_{S2} \quad [\text{Love Dispersion}] $$
