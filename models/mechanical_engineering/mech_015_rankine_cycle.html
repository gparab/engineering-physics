Steam Power Rankine Cycle

Mechanical Engineering

How It Works

The Rankine cycle is the fundamental operating thermodynamic cycle for steam turbine power generation plants. Subcooled liquid water is pressurized by a feed pump (1→2), vaporized and superheated at constant pressure inside the boiler across the liquid-vapor saturation dome (2→3), expanded isentropically through a steam turbine to drive an electrical generator (3→4), and fully condensed back into liquid inside a surface condenser (4→1).

Governing Equation
$$ W_{\text{net}} = (h_3 - h_4) - (h_2 - h_1) \quad\vert\quad \eta_{\text{th}} = \frac{W_{\text{net}}}{h_3 - h_2} \quad\vert\quad Q_{\text{in}} = \dot{m}(h_3 - h_2) $$
