Lambert's Problem Orbital Targeting

Astrodynamics

How It Works

Lambert's problem is the classical two-point boundary-value problem of determining the Keplerian orbit connecting two position vectors r₁ and r₂ in a specified time of flight Δt. Solving Lambert's problem yields the required departure velocity vector v₁, enabling ballistic interplanetary trajectory insertion, satellite rendezvous targeting, and mid-course trajectory correction.
