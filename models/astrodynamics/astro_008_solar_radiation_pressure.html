Solar Radiation Pressure & Sail Dynamics

Astrodynamics

How It Works

Solar radiation pressure provides continuous propellantless thrust by reflecting solar photons off high-reflectivity membrane sails. Photons transfer momentum Δp = 2E/c upon reflection. By articulating the sail pitch angle α relative to the incident sunlight vector, a tangential force component is produced that continuously raises orbital altitude in a logarithmic spiral.

Governing Equation
F_srp = (2 · P₀ · A / c) · (r₀ / r)² · cos²(α) , a_sail = F_srp / m
