Atmospheric Entry Aerobraking & Peak Heating

Astrodynamics

How It Works

Atmospheric entry converts the orbital kinetic energy of a hypersonic vehicle into intense aerodynamic heating through atmospheric drag. As atmospheric density increases exponentially with descending altitude, the spacecraft experiences a sharp deceleration pulse (peak g-load) and extreme convective and radiative heat flux at the blunt body stagnation point.

Governing Equation
$$ \frac{dv}{dt} = -\frac{\rho(z) v^2}{2 \beta} + g \sin\gamma \quad\vert\quad \dot{q}_{\text{stag}} = k \sqrt{\frac{\rho}{R_n}} v^3 $$
