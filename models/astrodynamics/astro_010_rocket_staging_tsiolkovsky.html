Multi-Stage Rocket Dynamics & Tsiolkovsky Equation

Astrodynamics

How It Works

The Tsiolkovsky rocket equation governs rocket ascent dynamics by balancing momentum conservation between accelerated propellant exhaust and vehicle mass. Multi-stage rockets overcome the severe logarithmic mass ratio barrier by jettisoning exhausted structural propellant tanks during ascent, progressively raising effective propellant mass fraction and yielding total orbital delta-V.

Governing Equation
Δv_total = Σ [v_e,i · ln(m_{0,i} / m_{f,i})] = Σ [g₀ · I_{sp,i} · ln(m_{0,i} / m_{f,i})]
