Hohmann Orbital Transfer & Delta-V Budget

Astrodynamics

How It Works

A Hohmann transfer is a two-impulse elliptical maneuver used to transfer a spacecraft between two coplanar circular orbits around a central gravitational body. The transfer ellipse is tangent to the departure orbit at periapsis and tangent to the target orbit at apoapsis, minimizing total fuel consumption. Two impulsive burns (Δv₁ and Δv₂) provide the necessary orbital energy shifts.

Governing Equation
Δv₁ = √(μ / r₁) · (√(2r₂ / (r₁ + r₂)) - 1) , Δv₂ = √(μ / r₂) · (1 - √(2r₁ / (r₁ + r₂)))
