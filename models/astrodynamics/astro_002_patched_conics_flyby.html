How It Works

The patched conics method breaks an interplanetary trajectory into a series of two-body Keplerian conic sections linked at the Sphere of Influence (SOI) boundaries. During a planetary flyby, the spacecraft executes a hyperbolic trajectory relative to the planet. While speed is conserved in the planet-centric frame, the turning angle δ redirects the hyperbolic excess velocity vector v_∞, adding or subtracting orbital velocity in the heliocentric frame.

Governing Equation
r_SOI = a · (m_p / M_sun)^(2/5) , Δv_helio = 2 · v_∞ · sin(δ / 2)
