Molniya High-Latitude Dwell Orbit

Astrodynamics

How It Works

A Molniya orbit is a highly elliptical 12-hour orbit inclined at the critical angle i = 63.435°, where the perigee precession rate caused by Earth's J2 oblateness vanishes identically. This freezes the orbital apogee over the northern hemisphere. According to Kepler's second law, satellites travel slowest near apogee, lingering for over eight hours per pass to provide persistent telecommunications over high-latitude polar regions.

Governing Equation
dω/dt = (3/4) · J₂ · (R_E / p)² · n · (5 cos²(i) - 1) = 0 [at i = 63.435°]
