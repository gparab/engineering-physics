Z-Pinch Magnetic Compression & Instabilities

Plasma Physics

How It Works

A Z-pinch uses a massive axial electric current flowing through a cylindrical plasma column to generate an intense azimuthal magnetic field. The resulting radial Lorentz force compresses the plasma to extreme temperatures and densities. However, ideal MHD sausage (m = 0) and kink (m = 1) instabilities rapidly develop, pinching the column into localized hot spots and helical coils.

Governing Equation
$$ (\vec{J} \times \vec{B})_r = -\frac{\partial P}{\partial r} \quad\vert\quad I^2 = \frac{8\pi}{\mu_0} N k_B (T_e + T_i) \quad [\text{Bennett}] $$
