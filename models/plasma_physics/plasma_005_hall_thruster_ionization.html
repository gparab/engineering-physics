Hall Thruster Ionization & E×B Drift

Plasma Physics

How It Works

A Hall effect thruster accelerates ions electrostatically while using a transverse magnetic field to trap electrons in a closed azimuthal E×B drift. Propellant gas (xenon) injected into the annular discharge channel undergoes electron impact ionization. Because the heavy positive ions have large Larmor radii, they remain unmagnetized and are efficiently accelerated axially downstream to produce propulsive thrust.

Governing Equation
$$ \vec{v}_E = \frac{\vec{E} \times \vec{B}}{B^2} \quad\vert\quad F = \dot{m}_i \sqrt{\frac{2 e V_{\text{acc}}}{m_i}} \quad\vert\quad I_{\text{sp}} = \frac{v_{\text{exit}}}{g_0} $$
