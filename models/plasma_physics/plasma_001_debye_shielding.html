Debye Shielding & Sheath Formation

Plasma Physics

How It Works

Debye shielding is the fundamental process by which a quasi-neutral plasma shields localized electrostatic potentials. Fast, highly mobile electrons respond to an inserted positive test charge by forming a screening cloud that causes the potential to decay exponentially rather than following a 1/r Coulomb drop. The characteristic screening length, known as the Debye length, defines the spatial boundary between individual particle interactions and collective plasma behavior.

Governing Equation
Φ(r) = (q / (4π ε₀ r)) · exp(-r / λ_D) , λ_D = √(ε₀ k_B T_e / (n_e e²))
