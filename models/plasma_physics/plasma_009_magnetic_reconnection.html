Sweet-Parker Magnetic Reconnection

Plasma Physics

How It Works

Magnetic reconnection is a fundamental plasma process where magnetic field lines of opposite polarity break and cross-connect within a thin resistive diffusion region. The rapid annihilation of magnetic flux releases vast quantities of stored magnetic energy into kinetic and thermal energy, powering solar flares, geomagnetic substorms, and tokamak sawtooth crashes.

Governing Equation
$$ v_{\text{in}} = \frac{v_A}{\sqrt{S}} \quad\vert\quad S = \frac{\mu_0 L v_A}{\eta} \quad [\text{Lundquist Number}] $$
