Langmuir Probe I-V Characteristics

Plasma Physics

How It Works

A Langmuir probe is a small conductive wire inserted into a plasma to measure local electron temperature, density, and plasma potential. As the applied voltage sweeps from negative to positive bias, the collected current traces a signature I-V curve comprising the ion saturation region, an exponential transition region dominated by thermal electrons overcoming the retarding potential, and the electron saturation region.

Governing Equation
$$ I(V) = I_{es} \exp\left(\frac{e(V - V_p)}{k_B T_e}\right) - I_{is} \quad (V < V_p) $$
