Magnetic Mirror Confinement & Loss Cone

Plasma Physics

How It Works

A magnetic mirror confines charged particles using an inhomogeneous magnetic field that converges at two choke points (throats). Because the first adiabatic invariant (magnetic moment μ = m v_⟂² / 2B) remains constant, a particle entering a region of increasing magnetic field experiences deceleration along the field line. If the pitch angle is sufficiently large, the parallel velocity drops to zero and the particle reflects; otherwise, it escapes through the loss cone.

Governing Equation
μ = m · v_⟂² / (2B) = const , sin(θ_loss) = √(B_min / B_max) = 1 / √R_m
