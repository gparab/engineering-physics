Collisionless Magnetosonic Shock Wave

Plasma Physics

How It Works

A collisionless shock wave forms when a supersonic plasma flow encounters an obstacle or counter-streaming plasma without relying on inter-particle Coulomb collisions. Instead, collective electromagnetic wave-particle interactions, micro-instabilities, and electrostatic potential jumps dissipate the upstream bulk kinetic energy into downstream thermal enthalpy and magnetic compression.

Governing Equation
M_A = v_flow / v_A > 1 , B₂ / B₁ = ρ₂ / ρ₁ = (γ + 1) M_A² / ((γ - 1) M_A² + 2)
