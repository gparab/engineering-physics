How It Works

The Maxwell model represents linear viscoelastic material behavior via an elastic Hookean spring and a viscous Newtonian dashpot coupled in series. When subjected to an instantaneous step strain, the spring responds instantaneously with initial stress, followed by irreversible molecular flow in the dashpot that exponentially relaxes internal stress over a characteristic relaxation time (τ = η / E).

Governing Equation
$$ \frac{d\sigma}{dt} + \frac{\sigma}{\tau} = E \frac{d\varepsilon}{dt} \quad\vert\quad \tau = \frac{\eta}{E} $$
