Arterial Hemodynamic Bifurcation Flow

Biomechanics Engineering

How It Works

Pulsatile blood flow through branching arterial bifurcations (such as the carotid artery) exhibits complex laminar velocity fields and spatial wall shear stress (WSS) variations. Near the apex divider, flow accelerates to produce elevated protective wall shear stress. Along the outer sinus bulbs, adverse pressure gradients generate secondary flow separation and low, oscillating wall shear stress prone to atheroma formation.
