Graphene Dirac Cone & Linear Dispersion

Nanotechnology

How It Works

Monolayer graphene features a relativistic electronic band structure in which the conduction and valence bands touch symmetrically at six isolated points in the hexagonal Brillouin zone, termed Dirac points. Near these vertices, charge carriers exhibit a strictly linear energy-momentum dispersion relation, behaving as massless Dirac fermions moving at a constant Fermi velocity v_F ≈ 10⁶ m/s.

Governing Equation
$$ E(\mathbf{k}) = \pm \hbar v_F |\mathbf{k} - \mathbf{K}| \quad\vert\quad v_F \approx 10^6\,\text{m/s} $$
