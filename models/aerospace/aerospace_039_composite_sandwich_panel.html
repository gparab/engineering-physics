Composite Sandwich Structural Flexure

Aerospace Engineering

How It Works

A composite sandwich panel achieves high flexural rigidity at minimal structural mass by bonding two thin, high-strength face sheets to a low-density core material. Under applied transverse bending moments, the outer face sheets carry nearly all normal stresses (upper skin under compression, lower skin under tension), while the lightweight honeycomb or foam core sustains transverse shear loads while holding the face sheets separated at a constant spacing.

Governing Equation
D = (E_f · t_f · d² / 2) + (E_f · t_f³ / 6) ≈ E_f · t_f · d² / 2 , τ_core ≈ V / (d · b)
