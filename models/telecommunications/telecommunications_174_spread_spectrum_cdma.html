Spread Spectrum (CDMA)

Telecommunications

How It Works

Direct Sequence Spread Spectrum (DSSS) and Code Division Multiple Access (CDMA) transmit data across hostile radio channels by multiplying narrowband information bits by a high-rate pseudorandom noise (PN) chipping sequence. Spreading the signal energy across a broad RF bandwidth depresses its power spectral density beneath the thermal noise floor, conferring severe immunity to narrowband jamming and multi-user interference, which are eliminated upon receiver despreading.

Governing Equation
s_{DSSS}(t) = d(t) · c(t) · cos(2π f_c t)   |   G_p = B_{spread} / B_{data} = T_b / T_c   |   ⟨c_i, c_j⟩ = δ_{ij}
