MIMO

Telecommunications

How It Works

Multiple-Input Multiple-Output (MIMO) telecommunications exploits spatial multipath propagation using multiple antennas at both transmitter and receiver. By decomposing the matrix radio channel H via singular value decomposition into parallel orthogonal spatial subchannels, MIMO multiplies spectral throughput without requiring extra transmit power or radio frequency bandwidth.

Governing Equation
y = H · x + n   |   C = log₂ det(I + (SNR / N_{Tx}) · H · H^H)   |   H = U · Σ · V^H
