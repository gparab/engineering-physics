How It Works

Creep is the time-dependent plastic deformation of materials subjected to constant mechanical stress at elevated homologous temperatures (T > 0.4 T_m). Under sustained tensile stress, vacancy diffusion drives cavitation void nucleation and growth along grain boundaries perpendicular to the tensile axis, eventually leading to void coalescence and tertiary intergranular creep fracture.

Governing Equation
$$ \frac{d\varepsilon}{dt} = A \sigma^n \exp\left(-\frac{Q_{\text{creep}}}{R T}\right) $$
