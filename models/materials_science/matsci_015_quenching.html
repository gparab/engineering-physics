Martensitic Quenching of Steel

Materials Science

How It Works

Quenching involves rapid non-equilibrium cooling of austenitic steel from elevated temperatures to suppress equilibrium diffusional transformations (pearlite/bainite). Because interstitial carbon atoms remain trapped within the iron lattice without time to diffuse, a cooperative shear restructuring transforms face-centered cubic austenite into hard, brittle, body-centered tetragonal martensite.

Governing Equation
$$ f_M = 1 - \exp(-\alpha (M_s - T)) $$
