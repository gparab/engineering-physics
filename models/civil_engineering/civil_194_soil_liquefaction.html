Cyclic Soil Liquefaction Dynamics

Civil Engineering

How It Works

Cyclic seismic shearing of saturated, loose granular soil triggers contractive grain rearrangement, generating high excess pore water pressures. When pore water pressure equals the total overburden stress, the effective stress drops to zero according to Terzaghi's principle. With contact friction lost, the soil loses all shear strength and undergoes transient fluidization, causing structural settlement and sand boils.

Governing Equation
$$ \sigma' = \sigma - u \quad\vert\quad r_u = \frac{u}{\sigma'_{v0}} \to 1.0 \quad (\text{Liquefaction}) $$
