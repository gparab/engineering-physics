Boussinesq Pavement Stress Bulb

Civil Engineering

How It Works

Boussinesq's classical elastostatic solution predicts the three-dimensional normal stress distribution within a semi-infinite elastic half-space subjected to a vertical point or contact patch load. Under heavy vehicular pavement wheel tires, vertical compressive stresses dissipate downward and laterally along axisymmetric isobaric surfaces termed stress bulbs. Rapid stress attenuation with depth guides flexible asphalt layer thickness design.

Governing Equation
σ_z = (3 · P) / (2π · z²) · [1 + (r / z)²]^(-5/2)
