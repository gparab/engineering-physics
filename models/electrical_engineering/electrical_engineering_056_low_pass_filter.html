Low-Pass Filter

Electrical Engineering

How It Works

A low-pass filter passes low-frequency electrical signals with negligible loss while progressively attenuating frequencies that exceed the designed cutoff frequency threshold f_c. In a basic passive RC topology, the series resistor and shunt capacitor form a voltage divider in which capacitive reactance drops inversely with frequency, shunting high-frequency noise and interference currents harmlessly to electrical ground.

Governing Equation
f_c = 1 / (2πRC)   |   |H(f)| = 1 / √(1 + (f / f_c)²)   |   ∠H(f) = -arctan(f / f_c)
