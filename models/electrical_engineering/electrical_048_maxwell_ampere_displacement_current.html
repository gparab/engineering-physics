Maxwell-Ampere Displacement Current

Electrical Engineering

How It Works

The Maxwell-Ampère law represents James Clerk Maxwell's historic completion of classical electrodynamics through the introduction of displacement current. When a capacitor charges or discharges, conduction current ceases at the plate boundaries, yet a magnetic field is observed surrounding the dielectric gap. Maxwell resolved this discontinuity by postulating that a time-varying electric flux generates an effective displacement current, ensuring total current continuity and enabling self-propagating electromagnetic waves.
