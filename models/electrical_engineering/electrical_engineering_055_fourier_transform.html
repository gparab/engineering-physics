Fourier Transform

Electrical Engineering

How It Works

The continuous Fourier transform is a central mathematical operator in signal processing that decomposes an arbitrary time-domain signal into a continuous spectrum of constituent sinusoidal frequencies. By projecting the temporal waveform onto an orthogonal continuum of complex exponential basis functions, the transform maps differential relationships into straightforward algebraic operations, exposing dominant resonant modes, system bandwidth, and harmonic frequency content.
