High-Pass Filter

Electrical Engineering

How It Works

A high-pass filter is a frequency-selective electrical network that allows high-frequency signal components to pass from input to output with minimal attenuation while progressively suppressing frequencies below a designated cutoff threshold. In a fundamental passive RC configuration, the capacitive reactance is inversely proportional to frequency, presenting high impedance to DC and low frequencies while acting as a near short-circuit to high frequencies. The corner frequency occurs where capacitive reactance equals resistance.

Governing Equation
f_c = 1 / (2πRC)   |   |H(f)| = (f / f_c) / √(1 + (f / f_c)²)   |   ∠H(f) = 90° - arctan(f / f_c)
