High Voltage Corona Discharge

Electrical Engineering

How It Works

High voltage corona discharge is an electrical ionization phenomenon occurring in regions of extreme electric field divergence, typically surrounding sharp pointed conductors or thin power transmission lines. When the local electric field gradient surpasses the dielectric breakdown strength of ambient air (~30 kV/cm at standard temperature and pressure), accelerated free electrons trigger Townsend avalanche ionization. The resulting electron transitions and ion recombination produce an audible hissing noise, ozone formation, and a characteristic violet luminous glow.
