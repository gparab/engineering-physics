Superconducting RF Cavity Q-Factor & Surface Resistance

Cryogenic Engineering

How It Works

Superconducting Radio-Frequency (SRF) cavities provide continuous multi-megavolt particle acceleration gradients with negligible energy dissipation. Operating at 2 K in superfluid liquid helium, high-purity niobium cavity walls exhibit microwave surface resistance R_s in the nano-ohm range, governed by the BCS theory. This tiny surface resistance yields extraordinary unloaded quality factors exceeding Q₀ > 10¹⁰.

Governing Equation
$$ Q_0 = \frac{G}{R_s} \quad\vert\quad R_{\text{BCS}}(T) \propto \frac{f^2}{T}\exp\left(-\frac{\Delta}{k_B T}\right) \quad\vert\quad P_{\text{diss}} = \frac{V_{\text{acc}}^2}{(R/Q) Q_0} $$
