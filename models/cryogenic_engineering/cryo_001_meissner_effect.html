Meissner Effect & Superconducting Magnetic Levitation

Cryogenic Engineering

How It Works

When cooled below its critical temperature T_c in an external magnetic field, a superconductor expels all internal magnetic flux (B = 0) through the spontaneous generation of lossless surface screening currents. Governed by the London equations, the magnetic field decays exponentially within the microscopic London penetration depth λ_L. The resulting diamagnetic repulsion stably levitates a permanent magnet above the superconductor.

Governing Equation
$$ B(x) = B_0 \exp\left(-\frac{x}{\lambda_L}\right) \quad\vert\quad \lambda_L = \sqrt{\frac{m}{\mu_0 n_s e^2}} $$
