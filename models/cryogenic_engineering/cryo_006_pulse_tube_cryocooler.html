Gifford-McMahon Pulse Tube Cryocooler

Cryogenic Engineering

How It Works

A pulse tube cryocooler is an advanced closed-cycle regenerative refrigerator that achieves cryogenic temperatures (down to 4 K) without any moving parts in the cold head, eliminating mechanical vibration and extending operating lifetimes. Oscillating helium gas pressure waves driven by a compressor expand through a regenerator and pulse tube. Phase shifting via an orifice and buffer reservoir produces an acoustic enthalpy flux that pumps heat from the cold tip to ambient.
