Type-II Superconductor Flux Pinning & Abrikosov Lattice

Cryogenic Engineering

How It Works

In Type-II superconductors exposed to magnetic fields between H_c1 and H_c2, magnetic flux penetrates the material in the form of quantized magnetic vortices (fluxons), each carrying exactly one flux quantum Φ₀ = h / 2e. Non-superconducting defects in the material microstructure pin these vortex cores in place, forming a regular triangular Abrikosov vortex lattice that locks the superconductor stably in three-dimensional space.

Governing Equation
$$ \Phi_0 = \frac{h}{2e} \approx 2.0678 \times 10^{-15}\text{ Wb} \quad\vert\quad \vec{F}_{\text{pinning}} \ge \vec{J} \times \vec{B} $$
