Josephson Junction Quantum Phase Dynamics

Cryogenic Engineering

How It Works

A Josephson junction consists of two superconducting electrodes separated by a nanometer-thin insulating barrier through which Cooper pairs tunnel coherently. Governed by the DC and AC Josephson relations, the supercurrent depends on the quantum phase difference φ across the barrier. When biased above the critical current I_c, the phase slips continuously, radiating high-frequency electromagnetic waves.

Governing Equation
$$ I_s = I_c \sin\varphi \quad\vert\quad \frac{d\varphi}{dt} = \frac{2e}{\hbar} V = \frac{2\pi}{\Phi_0} V $$
