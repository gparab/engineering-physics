Helium Dilution Refrigeration Cycle (3He-4He)

Cryogenic Engineering

How It Works

Helium dilution refrigeration enables continuous cooling down to millikelvin temperatures (below 10 mK) without mechanical moving parts in the cryogenic zone. Below 0.87 K, a mixture of Helium-3 and Helium-4 spontaneously separates into a concentrated ³He phase floating on top of a dilute ³He-in-⁴He phase. Endothermic evaporation of ³He atoms across this phase boundary absorbs latent heat of mixing, providing essential sub-kelvin cooling for quantum processors.

Governing Equation
$$ \dot{Q}_{\text{cool}} = 84 \, \dot{n}_3 T_{\text{mix}}^2 \quad [\text{J/s}] \quad\vert\quad T_{\text{base}} < 10\text{ mK} $$
