Fault Tree Analysis & System Reliability

Industrial & Systems Engineering

How It Works

Fault Tree Analysis (FTA) is a top-down deductive probabilistic risk assessment methodology that maps causal failure relationships. Starting from an undesirable catastrophic Top Event, Boolean logic gates (AND/OR gates) decompose systemic hazards into intersecting combinations of primary component failure rates.

Governing Equation
P(A ∪ B) = P(A) + P(B) - P(A ∩ B) , P(A ∩ B) = P(A) · P(B)
