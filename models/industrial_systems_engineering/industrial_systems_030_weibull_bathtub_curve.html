Weibull Reliability Hazard Bathtub Curve

Industrial & Systems Engineering

How It Works

Reliability engineering models component hazard and failure rates over operating life using the Weibull probability distribution. The classic bathtub curve integrates three distinct failure epochs: infant mortality with decreasing failure rate (shape parameter β < 1), useful life with constant random failure rate (β = 1), and age-related wear-out with increasing failure rate (β > 1).

Governing Equation
$$ h(t) = \frac{\beta}{\eta} \left(\frac{t}{\eta}\right)^{\beta - 1} \quad\vert\quad R(t) = \exp\left[-\left(\frac{t}{\eta}\right)^\beta\right] $$
