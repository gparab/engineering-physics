Step-Index Optical Waveguide Fiber

Optical Engineering

How It Works

A step-index optical fiber confines and guides optical signals within a cylindrical core surrounded by a lower-refractive-index cladding via total internal reflection. Light rays entering within the acceptance cone defined by the numerical aperture (NA) undergo low-loss zigzag reflections down the fiber length.

Governing Equation
$$ \mathrm{NA} = \sqrt{n_{\text{core}}^2 - n_{\text{clad}}^2} \quad\vert\quad V = \frac{2\pi a}{\lambda}\mathrm{NA} $$
