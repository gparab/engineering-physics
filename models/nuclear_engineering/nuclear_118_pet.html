Positron Emission Tomography (PET)

Nuclear Engineering

How It Works

Positron Emission Tomography (PET) is a functional molecular imaging modality utilizing radiotracers that undergo β⁺ radioactive decay. After emitting a positron that thermalizes over a sub-millimeter path, positron-electron annihilation converts rest mass into two back-to-back 511 keV gamma photons, detected in coincidence timing along a line of response (LOR).

Governing Equation
e^+ + e^- → 2γ (E_γ = 511 keV , θ = 180°)
