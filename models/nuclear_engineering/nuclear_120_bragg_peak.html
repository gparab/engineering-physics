Bragg Peak Radiation Dose Profile

Nuclear Engineering

How It Works

The Bragg peak characterizes the depth-dose deposition profile of energetic heavy charged particles (such as protons and carbon ions) traveling through matter. As the particle slows via inelastic Coulomb collisions with atomic electrons, its stopping power increases inversely with the square of its velocity, depositing the maximum ionizing radiation dose in a sharp peak just before coming to a complete rest.

Governing Equation
-dE / dx = (4π · z^2 · e^4) / (m_e · v^2) · n_e · [ln((2m_e v^2) / I) - β^2]
