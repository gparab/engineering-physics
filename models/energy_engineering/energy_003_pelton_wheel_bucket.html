Pelton Impulse Hydroelectric Turbine

Energy Engineering

How It Works

High-head hydroelectric power plants employ Pelton impulse turbines where high-velocity water jets from stationary nozzles strike double-cupped split runner buckets. The central splitter ridge divides the water jet symmetrically, turning the stream through nearly 180° to maximize momentum transfer to the rotating wheel. Maximum mechanical power output is achieved when the tangential bucket peripheral velocity equals half the jet velocity.

Governing Equation
$$ F_x = \dot{m}(v_{\text{jet}} - u)(1 - \cos\beta) \quad\vert\quad P_{\text{max}} = \frac{1}{2}\dot{m} v_{\text{jet}}^2 \quad (u = v_{\text{jet}} / 2) $$
