Compressed Air Cavern Energy Storage (CAES)

Energy Engineering

How It Works

Compressed Air Energy Storage (CAES) provides utility-scale bulk electrical energy storage by using off-peak electricity to drive multi-stage compressors, injecting dense air into deep underground salt caverns or porous aquifers. During peak demand periods, the high-pressure compressed air expands through recuperators and gas expanders to drive turbine-generators under polytropic thermodynamic work cycles.

Governing Equation
$$ W_{\text{poly}} = \frac{P_1 V_1}{n - 1}\left[1 - \left(\frac{P_2}{P_1}\right)^{\frac{n-1}{n}}\right] \quad\vert\quad P V^n = \text{const} $$
