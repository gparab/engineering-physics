Fickian Drug Diffusion in Tissue

Biomedical Engineering

How It Works

Targeted drug delivery in biomedical engineering is governed by molecular diffusion from a polymeric implant reservoir through extracellular tissue matrices. Fick's Second Law describes how the local drug concentration profile evolves over time in response to the Laplacian of the concentration gradient. Radial molecular transport establishes an expanding, diffuse concentration plume governed by the tissue diffusion coefficient (D).
