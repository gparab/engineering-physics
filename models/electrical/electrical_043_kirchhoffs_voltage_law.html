Kirchhoff's Voltage Law (Loop)

Electrical Engineering

How It Works

Kirchhoff's Voltage Law (KVL) represents the conservation of energy within a closed electrical circuit loop in the electrostatic or quasi-static limit. The directed line integral of the conservative electrostatic field around any closed loop is identically zero, meaning a test charge traversing the loop gains and loses equal amounts of potential energy. Consequently, the algebraic sum of all electromotive forces (voltage rises) around any closed loop precisely balances the sum of all resistive potential drops.
