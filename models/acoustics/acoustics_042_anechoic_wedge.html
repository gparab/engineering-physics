Anechoic Chamber Wedge Absorption

Acoustics Engineering

How It Works

Anechoic test chambers create simulated acoustic free-field conditions by lining walls with elongated, porous fibrous wedges. The geometric tapering of the wedges presents a continuous spatial impedance gradient to incoming sound, preventing the abrupt acoustic impedance step that causes reflections. Acoustic energy penetrates deep into the porous matrix, where viscous drag and thermal relaxation dissipate acoustic energy into heat.
