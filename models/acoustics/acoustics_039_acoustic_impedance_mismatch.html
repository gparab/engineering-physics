Acoustic Impedance Mismatch & Reflection

Acoustics Engineering

How It Works

When an acoustic pressure wave strikes a planar interface between two media of differing specific acoustic impedances (Z = ρ·c), acoustic continuity requirements cause energy partitioning into a reflected wave and a transmitted wave. The acoustic pressure reflection coefficient (R) and transmission coefficient (T) depend exclusively on the impedance ratio. Extreme impedance mismatches result in total acoustic reflection, creating the necessity of acoustic matching layers.

Governing Equation
R = (Z₂ - Z₁) / (Z₂ + Z₁) , T = (2 · Z₂) / (Z₂ + Z₁) , R_I = ((Z₂ - Z₁) / (Z₂ + Z₁))²
