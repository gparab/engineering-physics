Granular Media Filtration & Darcy's Law

Environmental Engineering

How It Works

Deep bed granular filtration purifies water through laminar porous flow governed by Darcy's law. Superficially applied water passes through sand and anthracite media grains where suspended matter is removed by inertial impaction and sedimentation. The continuous accumulation of retained flocs reduces media porosity, causing progressive hydraulic head loss across the filter bed.

Governing Equation
$$ v = -K \frac{dh}{dL} \quad\vert\quad h_L = f \frac{L}{d}\frac{v^2}{g}\left[\frac{1 - \varepsilon}{\varepsilon^3}\right] $$
