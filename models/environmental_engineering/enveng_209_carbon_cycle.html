Global Carbon Cycle Mass Balance

Environmental Engineering

How It Works

The global carbon cycle model tracks mass exchange fluxes among major planetary reservoirs: the atmosphere, terrestrial biosphere, surface oceans, and geological deposits. Conservation of mass dictates that changes in atmospheric carbon inventory equal the sum of anthropogenic fossil fuel emissions minus photosynthetic uptake by terrestrial vegetation and chemical dissolution into ocean waters.

Governing Equation
$$ \frac{dM_{\text{atm}}}{dt} = E_{\text{anthro}} - F_{\text{ocean}} - F_{\text{terrestrial}} \quad\vert\quad \sum_i \frac{dm_i}{dt} = 0 $$
