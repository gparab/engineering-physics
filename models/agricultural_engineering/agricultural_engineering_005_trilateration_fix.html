Precision Agriculture GNSS Trilateration

Agricultural Engineering

How It Works

Precision agricultural autosteer tractors and field sprayers determine real-time kinematic (RTK) coordinates through spherical satellite trilateration. Pseudorange measurements from at least four GNSS satellites establish intersecting spheres centered at known orbital ephemerides, solving for tractor coordinates (x, y, z) and receiver clock bias. Centimeter-level precision minimizes implement overlap and optimizes seed placement.

Governing Equation
ρ_i = √[(x - x_i)² + (y - y_i)² + (z - z_i)²] + c · Δt_rx
