Seed Comminution & Milling Fracture

Agricultural Engineering

How It Works

Mechanical grain milling and comminution reduce crop seeds into flour or animal feed through high-stress compressive and shearing roller forces. Fracture propagation initiates when internal tensile stress concentrates at pre-existing microcracks, exceeding the critical fracture toughness K_Ic. Bond's comminution law relates mechanical energy consumption to the reduction in particle size distribution.
