Capillary Drip Irrigation Wetting Bulb

Agricultural Engineering

How It Works

Drip irrigation delivers localized water directly to crop root zones, forming an axisymmetric wetted soil bulb governed by Richards' equation for unsaturated porous flow. Capillary matric suction pulls moisture laterally and upward against gravity, while hydraulic conductivity dictates downward drainage. Balancing the wetting pattern prevents deep percolation losses and maintains optimal soil moisture potential.
