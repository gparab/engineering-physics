Smith Chart (Telecom)

Telecommunications Engineering

How It Works

In telecommunication antenna and transmission line engineering, the Smith chart provides a comprehensive circular mapping of complex reflection coefficients and transmission line impedances. As a radio frequency signal propagates toward an antenna load, the normalized impedance rotates clockwise around the chart along a circle of constant Voltage Standing Wave Ratio (VSWR), enabling quarter-wave transformers and reactive stubs to eliminate signal reflections.

Governing Equation
$$ z_{\text{in}}(l) = \frac{z_L + j \tan(\beta l)}{1 + j z_L \tan(\beta l)} \quad\vert\quad \Gamma_{\text{in}} = \Gamma_L e^{-j 2\beta l} \quad\vert\quad \beta = \frac{2\pi}{\lambda} $$
